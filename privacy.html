Privacy policy

Kindling backups is used only by its owner, Shane de Jager, to store encrypted backups of their own self-hosted app in their own Google Drive.

It asks Google for the drive.file permission, so it can only see and change files it created itself. It cannot read anything else in your Drive.

The backups are encrypted before upload. No data is shared with anyone, sold or used for any other purpose.
